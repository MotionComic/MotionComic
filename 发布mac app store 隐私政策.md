Motion Comic Studio Writer 隐私政策
更新日期：2026

Motion Comic Studio Writer 是一款本地 Markdown 写作软件。
您创作的所有文稿文件均保存在您的 Mac 本地设备中。
本应用不会自动收集、上传、传输您的小说文稿、文本内容至任何远程服务器。

1. 数据存储
您创建、编辑的所有文档保存在本机。除非您主动手动操作，文稿不会向外发送。
2. 信息收集
本应用不会主动采集您的个人身份信息。
若您主动联系开发者寻求技术支持，您主动提供的信息仅用于处理您的问题。
3. 第三方服务
本软件未内置第三方统计、追踪SDK、广告模块。
4. 用户权利
您拥有在软件内创建的全部Markdown文档完整所有权，随时可以导出、删除本地文件。

如您对本隐私政策存在疑问，可联系开发者。

# English

Privacy Policy for Motion Comic Studio Writer

Last updated: 2026

Motion Comic Studio Writer is a local Markdown writing application.
All your manuscript files are stored locally on your Mac device.
This application DOES NOT automatically collect, upload or transmit your written content, personal manuscripts, text data to any remote server.

1. Data Storage
All documents you create and edit are saved locally on your computer. No content will be sent to external servers without your explicit manual operation.

2. Data We Collect
This app does not actively gather your personal information.
If you choose to contact support voluntarily, any information you send will only be used to reply to your request.

3. Third-party Services
This software does not embed third-party analytics, tracking SDKs or advertising modules.

4. Your Rights
You fully own all local Markdown files created within this application. You may copy, export or delete your files freely at any time.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
