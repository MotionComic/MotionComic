Privacy Policy for Motion Comic Studio Writer

Last updated: 2026

Motion Comic Studio Writer is a local Markdown writing application. All your manuscript files are stored locally on your Mac device. This application DOES NOT automatically collect, upload or transmit your written content, personal manuscripts, text data to any remote server.

1. Data Storage

All documents you create and edit are saved locally on your computer. No content will be sent to external servers without your explicit manual operation.

2. Data We Collect

This app does not actively gather your personal information. If you choose to contact support voluntarily, any information you send will only be used to reply to your request.

3. Third-party Services

This software does not embed third-party analytics, tracking SDKs or advertising modules.

4. Your Rights

You fully own all local Markdown files created within this application. You may copy, export or delete your files freely at any time.

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at 10226093@qq.com or visit our Support page.
